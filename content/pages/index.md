<h1 class="site-heading">Royal City Suds</h1>

Royal City Suds is a mobile car washing service serving downtown Guelph. No drive-throughs, no waiting. I come to you with everything needed to make your car spotless.

---

## Services

**Exterior wash $30**:
A thorough hand wash of your vehicle's exterior. Wheels, windows, and body. I'll leave it looking sharp without you leaving your driveway.

**Interior clean $50**:
Full interior vacuum and wipe-down. Seats, dash, cup holders, door panels. You'll feel like you're driving it off the lot.

**Full detail $75**:
The complete package. Interior clean plus exterior wash, done in one visit. Save $5 and get it all handled at once.

---

## How it works

1. **Email to book.** Send your address, preferred wash, and a couple of days that work. I'll reply shortly with available times.
2. **I come to you.** I show up at your downtown Guelph location with all the supplies.
3. **You get a clean car.** A spotless vehicle waiting when I'm done.
