<h1 class="site-heading">Royal City Suds</h1>

Royal City Suds is a mobile car washing service serving downtown Guelph. No drive-throughs, no waiting. Henry shows up wherever you are with everything needed to make your car spotless.

---

## Services

**Exterior wash $30**:
A thorough hand wash of your vehicle's exterior. Wheels, windows, and body. Leave it looking sharp without leaving your driveway.

**Interior clean $50**:
Full interior vacuum and wipe-down. Seats, dash, cup holders, door panels. You'll feel like you're driving it off the lot.

**Full detail $75**:
The complete package. Interior clean plus exterior wash, done in one visit. Save $5 and get it all handled at once.

---

## How it works

1. **Email to book.** Send your address, preferred wash, and a couple of days that work. Henry will reply shortly with available times.
2. **Henry comes to you.** He shows up at your downtown Guelph location with all the supplies.
3. **You get a clean car.** A spotless vehicle waiting when he's done.

## About

Hi, I'm Henry, and I started Royal City Suds to give downtown Guelph a better way to get their car cleaned. No big company, no hidden fees. Just reliable, careful work from someone who takes pride in what he does. I bring all my own equipment and supplies. All you have to do is let me know when and where.
